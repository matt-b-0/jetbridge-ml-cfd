# Initial-case assumptions and validation limitations

- Controlled case only, no manikins. Experimental multi-plane measurements were collected sequentially.
- Real inlet diffuser geometry is simplified to a uniform full-face flux inlet; this affects the near-inlet flow.
- Model imposes total volumetric supply rate from HVAC; the experimental jet bridge may exchange flow with surroundings.
- The experimental SWEMA devices measure speed, not full velocity direction.
- The first mesh uses uniform cells; turbulence wall-functions and y+ are not yet verified.
- CFD-derived sensor density tests measure fidelity to a CFD solution, not accuracy relative to the physical experiment.
- Compare model and measurements with identical experimental configuration and coordinates; validate source data quality and temporal averaging windows.
